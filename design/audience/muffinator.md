Bio
This madman has a screw loose. They see faction as an avenue for character progression, and keep going until the faction cannot possibly get any better. They've always wanted to maximize all factions possible. They have attempted to tackle the challenge with spreadsheets, or even pencil and paper. They are unhappy with the difficulty optimizing conflicting factions with existing tools, it really seems like there should be a better way.

Pain points
 - Groupings of faction hits are not differentiated
 - Adding actions in the wrong order will undo previous work
 - Doesn't need a 1 shot tool, plans that aren't durable and adjustable are useless

Needs
 - Clarity on secondary faction impacts
 - Flexibility on planned action order
 - To revisit plans created earlier
