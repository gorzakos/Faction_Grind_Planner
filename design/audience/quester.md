Bio
This player has a goal, and faction requirements are the obstacle. They want an item from a quest, but that quest has faction requirements. They want to know how to meet those requirements as soon as possible. Damage to other factions may be a factor, and sometimes they may want to repair the aftermath, but usually the more important factor is time.

Pain Points
 - How long does it take? Actions without time estimates aren't very helpful
 - What's the damage?

Needs
 - Some means to evaluate primary impacts by time
 - Clarity on secondary faction impacts
